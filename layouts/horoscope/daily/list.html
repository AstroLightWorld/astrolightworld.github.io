HOW IT WORKS

Calculated from the sky, not randomly generated.

Each daily horoscope begins with calculated planetary positions for the current date. AstroLight then evaluates planetary sign relationships, active aspects and predefined interpretation rules for the selected Sun sign.

01

Planetary positions

Celestial positions are calculated using Astronomy Engine.

02

Astrological relationships

Planetary signs and aspects are evaluated relative to the reader's Sun sign.

03

Daily interpretation

Deterministic interpretation rules turn those signals into a daily reading.
