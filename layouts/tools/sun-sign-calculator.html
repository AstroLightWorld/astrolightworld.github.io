METHODOLOGY

How the Sun Sign is calculated

AstroLight uses astronomical solar longitude rather than relying only on a fixed date-range table.

01

Birth date

The selected calendar date is converted into an astronomical calculation time.

02

Solar position

Astronomy Engine calculates the geocentric position of the Sun.

03

Tropical longitude

The Sun's longitude is measured from 0° through 360° in the Tropical zodiac.

04

Zodiac sign

Each 30° sector corresponds to one Western Tropical zodiac sign.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR SUN SIGN

What does the Sun Sign represent?

Traditional astrology

In Western astrology, the Sun is traditionally associated with identity, vitality, purpose, self-expression and conscious direction.

Tropical zodiac

The Western Tropical zodiac divides the ecliptic into twelve equal 30° signs beginning at the traditional Aries point.

Boundary dates

The Sun changes zodiac signs at a precise astronomical moment, and that moment varies from year to year. This date-only calculator uses 12:00 UTC as its calculation instant. If you were born close to a sign boundary, use the Birth Chart calculator with your exact birth time and place for a more precise result.
