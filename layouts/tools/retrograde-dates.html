HOW IT IS CALCULATED

Calculation first. Interpretation second.

01

Planetary position

AstroLight calculates Mercury's geocentric ecliptic longitude for sampled UTC times.

02

Apparent motion

We measure how Mercury's longitude changes over time and identify reversals in apparent direction.

03

Station timing

The transition is refined around the point where the calculated apparent longitudinal motion changes direction.

04

Traditional meaning

The current motion, Mercury's sign and proximity to a station are translated into a clearly labeled astrological reflection.

Important: Retrograde is an apparent change in geocentric motion caused by the relative orbital motion of Earth and Mercury. It does not mean Mercury physically reverses its orbit. Astrological interpretations are traditional symbolism, not scientific causation.
