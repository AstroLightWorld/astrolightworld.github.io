# Contact AstroLight

Found an incorrect calculation, broken link, unclear explanation or useful topic that should be added?

AstroLight welcomes corrections and constructive feedback.

## What to include

When reporting a technical issue, please include:

- the page or calculator where the issue occurred;
- the input values needed to reproduce it, excluding information you do not want to share;
- the result you expected;
- the result you received;
- your browser and device if the issue appears visual.

For content corrections, quote the relevant heading or sentence and explain what should be changed.
